About Us

Dajavshne — booking made simple

Dajavshne was created by AI Solutions Georgia LLC with one main idea: booking made simple and accessible for everyone. We bring together diverse experiences — from entertainment venues to restaurants, sports, and healthcare services — into one platform, so users can search for and book their desired service in just a few seconds.

Our Mission

Our mission is to simplify everyday life and help you plan your time effectively. Whether you want to spend an evening with friends, book workout time, get a medical consultation, or reserve a restaurant table — Dajavshne is one platform for all your needs.

What We Offer to Users

  • Unified Space: All services on one platform, just a few clicks away.
  • Transparent Terms: Clear prices, times, and conditions.
  • Security: Secure payments and reliable partners.
  • Time-Saving: No more calls and waiting — everything online.
  • Trusted Information: Real reviews and experiences from other users.

What We Offer to Partners

  • New Customer Acquisition: The platform increases visibility and boosts sales.
  • Efficient Management: Fast booking processing and control.
  • Analytics: Data that helps business growth and better understanding of customers.
  • Marketing Support: Promotions, campaigns, and direct communication with customers.
  • Reliability: Transparent terms and secure payments.

Why Dajavshne?

  • Because we bring all experiences into one platform.
  • Because we care about both customer comfort and partner business growth.
  • Because our goal is to make booking in Georgia and beyond as simple as clicking one button.

Our Vision

Today, Dajavshne offers booking for entertainment venues, but this is only the beginning. We plan to expand the platform into multiple directions — restaurants, health and beauty services, sports activities, events, and much more. We believe that Dajavshne will soon become the platform where everything is bookable — Don’t call, just book it!
