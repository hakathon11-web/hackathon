Refund Policy

1. General Provisions

The Refund Policy defines the conditions under which Dajavshne (AI Solutions Georgia LLC) provides refunds to users for payments made.

2. Booking Cancellation by User

  • A user may cancel a booking before it is confirmed by the partner – in this case, the amount is fully refunded.
  • After confirmation by the partner, refunds depend on the partner’s policy and may be partial or not provided at all.

3. Booking Cancellation by Partner

  • If the partner cancels a confirmed booking, the user will receive a full refund of the paid amount.
  • The company has the right to refund the amount directly through the payment channel (e.g., card, iPay).

4. Special Cases

  • A full refund will be issued if the service could not be provided due to technical failure, force majeure, or improper action by the company/partner.
  • If the user does not show up at the booked time, no refund will be issued unless the partner independently approves it.

5. Refund Procedure

Refund requests must be sent to: support@dajavshne.io

The request must include: booking number, user’s name, and proof of payment.

The company is obliged to verify the information and issue the refund no later than 10 business days after processing the request.
